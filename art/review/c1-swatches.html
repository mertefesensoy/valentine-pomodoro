The Sticker Diary: theme tokens

Phase 1, C1. Two free themes with the same roles: Strawberry Milk (light) and Midnight Plum (dark). Every text and background pair the interface will draw is listed under its theme, with its contrast ratio. Text needs 4.5:1; large text, icons and non-text marks (the ring, input boundaries) need 3:1.

The ratios on this page come from the same function and the same file that CI checks (npm run check:contrast), so what you approve is what ships. The tomato disc is a placeholder showing the sticker edge and shadow; Pomo & Doro come in C3.

All 52 pairs pass

One choice for you: the primary button

A pastel strawberry can't carry white text at 4.5:1. So either the fill stays soft and the label is cocoa (proposed: it reads like stationery and keeps the deeper pink for text and the ring), or the fill goes deep and the label is white (more conventional, louder).

A · Proposed
Cocoa on pastel strawberry · 6.76:1
B · Alternative
White on deep strawberry · 6.13:1
