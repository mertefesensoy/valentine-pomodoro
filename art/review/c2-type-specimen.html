Recommendation: Baloo 2, embedded with D1

  • Of your three, it is the one that passes and has a voice. Its captions and timer read like stickers, which is the Sticker Diary's signature. Keep it to the timer, screen titles and sticker captions; everything else stays in the system face, as DESIGN.md already says.
  • Turkish: all twelve letters, plus Turkish-aware i-forms. Digits: they hold still through tabular figures, one React Native prop (fontVariant: ['tabular-nums']), checked on the iPhone in D1's device check. Size: 58 KB per weight, subset to Latin and Turkish.
  • M PLUS Rounded 1c is the calm alternative: its digits are fixed-width with no prop at all, but its Latin is plainer, and its wide colon opens a gap in the timer.
  • Nunito passes too, but it is one of the most-used rounded faces in app and dashboard templates, so it would make the app look like many others.
  • Fredoka fails: it has no İ, Ğ, ğ, Ş or ş, and its digits change width as the timer counts.
  • Whichever you pick, the embedded subset drops the fi and fl ligatures, so a Turkish i always keeps its dot.
