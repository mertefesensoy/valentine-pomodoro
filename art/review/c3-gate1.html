Pomo & Doro: the personality map

VoiceWarm, gentle, encouraging, with a quiet sense of humour. Never guilt, never pressure.
AudienceUniversity-age couples, 18 and over, mostly long-distance; Turkey is a core market.
What they already loveJapanese and Korean stationery: sticker sheets, washi tape, study planners; soft flat characters on paper; café drinks like strawberry milk and matcha.
RegisterCute enough to be loved, grown-up enough for a lecture hall. Playful, never babyish.
Pomo, the steady oneRound and upright, with one curl that springs up when an idea lands. Starts things. Keeps the minutes: the top of the colon. Strawberry.
Doro, the warm oneA soft dome that settles, sinks a little when sleepy and wobbles when happy. First to cheer. Keeps the seconds: the bottom of the colon. Matcha.
TogetherEquals and partners, never parent and child: the same visual weight (within 10 %). Strawberry and matcha are two flavours of one sweet, so the pair isn't coded by gender.
NeverA tomato, an animal (the category's habit), sulking, crying or nagging, a gendered cue, a babyish face.

Rendering style

Construction, locked

EyesOn the eye line at 52 % of body height; 26 % of body width apart; each 7 % of width, an upright oval (1 : 1.25) with a shine at the upper right.
Cheeks11 % below the eye line, just outside the eyes; 12 % × 6 % of width; blush at 55 %.
MouthCentred, 9 % of width, 8.5 % below the eye line. Expressions change the mouth and eyes, never the eye line or the spacing.
Sticker edgeA 20-unit white die-cut edge (about 6 % of Pomo's width) and a soft shadow tinted from the theme.
ColoursPomo strawberry (primary), Doro matcha (the success leaf, one step deeper so it holds on cream), cocoa for the eyes and mouth (ink).
