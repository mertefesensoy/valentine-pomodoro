The mark in context, and the full cast

Phase 1, C3, gate 2. The app icon you picked in all three appearances, the splash, and the mark where people will meet it. Then Pomo & Doro in all five poses, the icon set, and every asset against its budget. Numbers on the mock-ups are examples, not data.

The app icon

1024 × 1024, opaque RGB with no alpha channel (App Store Connect rejects transparency), square and unmasked so iOS draws the corners and its own glass effects. Flattened, because Icon Composer needs a Mac; the layers are kept apart in the sources for a layered icon later.

The splash

Static, never animated (HIG review H-2). One choice for you: Apple's launch-screen guidance says not to put a logo on it unless the first screen shows one, and the Classic page doesn't. It is guidance, not an App Review rule, so your brief ("the splash built from the logo") can win. The third phone is the alternative.

The mark in context

Pomo & Doro

Every pose keeps the locked construction: the eye line, the eye gap and the proportions never move; only the eyes, mouth, curl and a gentle squash change. Nobody sulks: sleepy is the lowest they go.

The icon set

46 icons on the 24 pt grid, 2 pt rounded strokes outlined by VectorCraft. The four tabs also come filled (Apple prefers filled tab icons), exported as template PNGs at 50 and 75 px, with real holes, since the tab bar draws alpha only:
