Logo concepts

Phase 1, C3. Four ideas for the mark, in black and white only, because a weak idea can't be rescued by colour. Each one tells one story and comes with the case for it, its risks, and what Pomo & Doro would become. Pick one; colour, the app icon, the splash and the mark in context come after.

Concept 1 of 4

Same Page

Two pages of one open diary, leaning in.

Why it fits
A couple who studies together is, quite literally, on the same page. The open diary says study and stays inside the Sticker Diary; the two pages are the two of you, apart at the edges and bound at the spine.
Wit and warmth
The idiom made literal. The spine is the one thing they share.
The characters
Pomo & Doro become two pages. Sleepy is a dog-eared corner pulled up like a blanket; celebrating is the pages fanning like wings.
Risks
An open book is the most common study symbol, so the lean and the faces carry the difference. Smiling-book clip art exists; the final drawing needs its own page curve.

Concept 2 of 4

Lean on Me

Two books that hold each other up.

Why it fits
Take one away and the other falls; together they stand. That is what a study partner does on a long night, and the A-shape they make reads at any size.
Wit and warmth
Two books on a shelf, behaving like a couple.
The characters
Pomo & Doro become two books: open while focusing, lying flat on a break, one napping on the other.
Risks
At small sizes it can pass for a library or a "Books" category icon. The shelf line may go in the final drawing.

Concept 3 of 4

The Colon

The two of you live between the minutes and the seconds.

Why it fits
In 24:59 the colon is the one mark that never changes. Here it is the two of you, with every minute held between you. It also splits the word in two, pomo:doro, which is how the lettering would work.
Wit and warmth
The most-seen character in the app turns out to be them.
The characters
Pomo & Doro become two round dots with faces.
Risks
Alone, two dots are generic, so this concept leans on its lettering, with the dots as the small-size mark. Round blob mascots are common.
pomo doro

Lettering sketch in Baloo 2, the face you picked: the colon is the mark. The final wordmark waits for the public name.

Concept 4 of 4

Red Thread

One thread ties the two of you, however far apart.

Why it fits
An East Asian legend says an invisible red thread joins the people who are meant to meet. It may stretch or tangle, but it never breaks. That is long-distance study in a single line, and when colour arrives the thread becomes the brand red.
Wit and warmth
Below about 30 px the two heads and the thread read as one smiling face.
The characters
Pomo & Doro become two round friends on one thread: taut while focusing, looped on a break, tied in a bow to celebrate, wound into a pillow when sleepy.
Risks
It says nothing about studying, which rule 8 allows. At middle sizes it can read as earbuds, so the thread has to stay thick enough for 20 px.

My recommendation: Same Page

It is the only concept that carries all three of your words at once: romantic (the two of you, leaning in), couples (two pages, one spine) and studying (an open diary). It also holds at 20 px better than any other. Red Thread is the more romantic and more unusual choice, if you're happy for the mark to say nothing about studying.

Whichever you pick, the colour app icon will show it as a die-cut sticker on a Strawberry Milk page, so the icon belongs to the Sticker Diary too.
