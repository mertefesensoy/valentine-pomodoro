The mark

Two pages of one diary, leaning in. Apart at the edges, bound at the spine: a couple on the same page.

An open diary as a die-cut sticker: strawberry pages in a cocoa cover, with cocoa faces (the owner's change, 2026-10-30). It never shows a clock, a heart or a tomato, the category's habits (ADR-0047).

The system face for everything else, following Dynamic Type.

Pomo & Doro

  • Pomo, the steady one: a round strawberry with one curl. Doro, the warm one: a wide matcha dome. Equals, the same visual weight.
  • Together they are the colon in the timer. Flat fills, a white die-cut edge, a soft tinted shadow.
  • The construction is locked: eye line at 52 %, eyes 26 % apart. Poses change the eyes, mouth and curl, never the build. They never sulk; sleepy is the lowest they go.

Voice and lettering

Warm, gentle, encouraging, with a quiet sense of humour. Never guilt, never pressure. Sentence case.
