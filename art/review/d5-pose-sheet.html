Pomo & Doro, ready to show people around

Phase 1, D5. The new faces and gestures for the tutorial, drawn in VectorCraft on the shipped construction, and how they move. Each character is a small kit of layers (the body, a hand, the eyes, the mouth), so a blink swaps only the eyes, talking swaps only the mouth, and a new expression crosses in while the body squashes. Everything here runs on the real drawings. Nothing is in the app yet.

The motion

The numbers the build will use, as this page plays them. Every move is about the feet, the bottom of the die-cut edge, and keeps the area: when the height scales by s, the width scales by 1 / s.

MotionHowUnder Reduce Motion
BlinkEyes closed for 110 ms, every 2.8 to 5.2 s at random; one blink in 5 is a double, 140 ms apart. Only on open-eyed faces, never mid-change.None
TalkingThe line writes on at one character every 45 ms (22.2 a second), pausing 150 ms after a comma and 300 ms after a stop inside the line. The kit

44 layers, 22 per character, all on the shipped 512 artboard, so they stack exactly. The body is the shipped silhouette with its face taken off; every face sits on the C3 grid (Doro's is Pomo's scaled by 13.7 / 12.3, as the shipped faces are). Each layer is 266 to 2,828 bytes, and the heaviest expression with all its frames is 5.5 KB, against 30 KB.

  1. The die-cut edge (white), which carries the lift.
  2. A hand, only for a wave or a point: a nub in the body's colour with its own edge.
  3. The fill and the cheeks. Drawn over the hand, it hides the hand's root, so the nub grows out of the body inside one edge.
  4. The eyes, then the mouth.

The feet (the transform origin) are at (256, 478) for Pomo and (256, 476) for Doro. Every drawing stays inside the artboard: the outermost points of all 44 layers span x 1 to 511 and y 35 to 478 of 0 to 512. Squash, breath and tilt are transforms of the whole sticker, which don't clip.

For you to decide

  1. The hands. C3 drew Pomo & Doro with no limbs. A wave and a point need something, so this kit adds a small nub in the body's colour that grows out only for those two gestures and goes away after. Keep the nubs? The limb-free way would be a lean towards the control and the eyes looking at it, with the ribbon doing all the pointing.
  2. The faces. Is each expression right, or should any be redrawn: softer, bigger, a different eye or mouth?
  3. The motion. Does the squash on each change, the blink and the breath feel right, or too much, or too little?
