THE ROLL

The bookmark ribbon

The diary’s strawberry ribbon, the one on the welcome’s closed diary, drops to whatever the pair are talking about and marks your place. They stand at its tip, big, taking turns; the bubble floats above them.

  • WHAT IT DOES WELL
    · One continuous thread from the welcome through every step: the ribbon is the tour.
    · Its tip points without a separate arrow, at a control or down at the tab bar.
    · The pair stand right beside the opening, big enough to carry the moment.
  • WHAT TO WATCH
    · The ribbon crosses the page, so each step needs a clear line to its target.
    · At AX sizes the ribbon shortens to a tab above the bottom panel (T-2).
