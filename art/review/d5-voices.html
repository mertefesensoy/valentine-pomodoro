How Pomo & Doro talk

Phase 1, D5. The babble the pair speak in while their lines write on in the tutorial: a few made-up syllables each, synthesised in SoundCraft from its built-in synths, with no samples, no human voice and no words. Please listen on your phone's speaker as well as on headphones.

Lines in
If you hear nothing on an iPhone, check the ring switch: the app itself will stay quiet on silent.

Pomo

Pomo

Higher and bouncier. A square-wave voice that springs up into each note, in short syllables. A syllable on every 3rd letter, about 7.4 a second.

The English line: 16 syllables over 3.3 s, at −18.0 LUFS. Syllables 2.5–2.9 KB of 25 KB, peaks −9.8 dBFS at most, brightness (spectral centroid) 791–1,292 Hz.

Doro

Doro

Lower and rounder. A triangle-wave voice that settles gently onto each note, in longer syllables. A syllable on every 4th letter, about 5.6 a second.

The English line: 14 syllables over 3.5 s, at −18.0 LUFS. Syllables 3.1–3.7 KB of 25 KB, peaks −9.4 dBFS at most, brightness (spectral centroid) 493–867 Hz.

Level

A little below the app's other sounds, so the babble sits under them. Each syllable is brought to one loudness, then each voice is set so its sample line measures −18.0 LUFS on the same meter as D4's sounds (the loudest 400 ms): about 3.8 LU under them. Every peak stays under D4's −1 dBFS.

Bars run from −30 to −10 LUFS. One step of 1 LU is about the smallest change most people notice.

Pace

The tutorial didn't have a write-on speed yet, so this is the proposal, the same one the pose sheet plays.

WhatProposed
Write-onOne character every 45 ms: 22.2 a second, about adult reading speed, so the line keeps up with the eye
Pauses150 ms after a comma; 300 ms after a full stop, question or exclamation mark inside the line
SyllablesPomo on every 3rd letter (about 7.4 a second), Doro on every 4th (about 5.6); the count starts again after each pause, and the last syllable rises for a question and falls otherwise
A full lineThe longest allowed (90 characters in English, 110 in Turkish) takes about 4.1 s and 5.0 s plus pauses. A tap on the bubble finishes it at once

For you to decide

  1. The voices. Do they sound like Pomo and Doro: cute, clearly not a person, Pomo higher and bouncier, Doro lower and rounder? Should either be softer, brighter, faster or a different pitch?
  2. The level. Is the babble at the right loudness next to focus start, or should it sit higher or lower?
  3. The pace. Is 22.2 characters a second right for the write-on, or should it be faster or slower?
