## 1. Problem / Motivation

The owner asked for the whole branding of the app in one folder, "just like we did in SuperconducTED and Cassandra Collective" (`Cassandra-Collective/.github/brand`): `eps/`, guides, PDFs, PNGs, sources, SVGs, tools, web files, a brand licence and a README. Stage C made the identity (the Same Page mark, Pomo & Doro, the palette, Baloo 2, the identity sheet), but its files live in the app's art pipeline (`art/`), with no lockups, no print formats, no web set and no licence.

**The owner's decisions (2026-10-30):**
- the lockups set the working name, **"Valentine Pomodoro"**, in Baloo 2, so a rename is a one-line change plus a rebuild
- the brand files are licensed **CC BY-ND 4.0**, as Cassandra's are
- the tagline is **"Study together, wherever you are."**

## 3. Implementation Approach

1. **One source of truth.** `art_geometry.py` holds the drawings Stage C made (the mark's pages, faces and edge; the app icon; Pomo & Doro's bodies, faces and poses), moved verbatim from the drafting tools outside the repo. `build_brand.py` owns every other number: the versions, the lockups, the cards and the guide figures, with colours read from `src/theme/palette.json`. For each asset it writes an SVG source, probes it (`object.expand.info`), then runs one `vectorcraft-cli run` that sets the File Info, expands strokes into fills, saves the `.vectorcraft` source and exports every format. **`check_brand.py` asserts that 20 of the brand's source drawings (the four marks, the three app icons, the twelve characters, the web icon) are byte for byte the app's own `art/src` files,** so the app and the brand can't drift.
2. **The wordmark is shaped, not typed.** `wordmark.py` shapes the text with HarfBuzz (kerning, the font's own spacing) from Baloo 2 at a fixed weight and writes each glyph's outline as a path. No font has to be installed in VectorCraft, and no file carries live text. **Weight: Baloo 2 Bold (700), lower case, "valentine pomodoro"**, per the C5 lettering style; the owner can overrule it at the gate.
3. **The variants are C5's four, with C5's names** (amended before the build, see §5): `colour` (the cocoa sticker: cocoa pages, milk faces, white die-cut edge; light grounds), `reversed` (strawberry pages, plum faces, milk edge; dark grounds), `single-cocoa` and `single-white` (one ink, the faces cut out as even-odd holes). They are the same four drawings as `art/src/identity/mark-*.svg`, and the check holds them to it. **There is no small mark:** below the 24 px minimum the app icon carries the brand (C5), so the favicons are the app icon.
4. **The lockups:** horizontal (mark, then the wordmark on one line) and stacked (the mark over the wordmark). There's no compact lockup, since there's no descriptor to drop.
5. **The characters:** Pomo & Doro's five poses and their single-colour versions, as SVG, PNG and source only. EPS and PDF of mascots nobody prints would only add file count.
6. **Determinism:** VectorCraft stamps the time into PNGs (a `tEXt` chunk, absent once File Info is set, still stripped if it appears), `.vectorcraft` files (`created`/`modified`, pinned by saving with `modified: 0`; gzip's own timestamp is already 0), PDFs (`D:` dates, XMP dates, a random trailer `/ID` and random XMP `DocumentID`/`InstanceID`) and EPS files (`%%CreationDate`). The build pins each to a constant of the same length; the four PDF identifiers become one hash of the file with them blanked, so the cross-reference offsets stay valid. Two builds match byte for byte, and `check_brand.py --rebuild` proves it.
7. **The checks**, over all files, not a sample:
   - every expected file exists, and nothing else is there
   - the brand's drawings equal the app's `art/src` files
   - every SVG is well-formed, has its title and a viewBox, no `<text>`, `<image>`, `<script>`, `<style>`, `<foreignObject>` or external references, and only its version's colours
   - every PNG has its size and colour type (RGBA for transparent art, RGB for tiles, cards and the guide) and no build time
   - every EPS paints only with `k`, only at the values recorded for its version's colours, with no RGB or grey operator in the page
   - every PDF has one page, the artboard's MediaBox and pinned dates; every source has its dates pinned
   - the palette's pairs (both themes) and the brand's own pairs meet their floors, and `wcag.py` agrees with `src/theme/contrast.ts` (run through Node) on all 57
8. **CMYK** is measured, not computed: each build exports one scratch EPS with a swatch per colour, reads back the `k` values and stops if any differs from the `EPS_CMYK` table recorded in `build_brand.py`.

## 4. Mathematical / Statistical Details

- **Contrast:** the WCAG 2 formula of ADR-0045 (`wcag.py` mirrors `src/theme/contrast.ts`; `check_brand.py` asserts the two agree on the palette).
- **CMYK:** not computed. The values are read back from VectorCraft's EPS export ("Generic CMYK (SWOP-like)"), as the Cassandra kit does. They are approximations, unproofed; no Pantone references are defined.
- **Units.** H is the height of the mark's frame (576 on the 1024 grid; the frame is 784 × 576). Baloo 2 Bold's top of "l" is $a = 0.6596$ em and its x-height (OS/2) is $x = 0.46$ em.
- **Lockups.** For a letter height $\ell$ (in H), the type size is $s = \ell / a$ and the x-height is $x s$.
  - Horizontal: $\ell = 0.34$, so $s = 0.5155\,H$ and the x-height is $0.237\,H$; the gap is $0.24\,H$; the band from the top of "l" to the baseline is centred on $H/2$. Width $= 784/576 + 0.24 + w_\text{ink} = 6.273\,H$.
  - Stacked: $\ell = 0.20$, so $s = 0.3032\,H$ and the x-height is $0.1395\,H$; $0.14\,H$ from the frame to the top of "l"; baselines $0.92\,s$ apart. The width is the widest line ("pomodoro", $1.395\,H$); the height is $1.676\,H$.
- **Clear space:** X = 0.25 H on every side (C5).
- **Minimum sizes.** The mark: 24 px wide on screen, 10 mm in print (the C5 test), so $H_\min = 24 \cdot 576/784 = 17.6$ px and $7.35$ mm. A lockup needs its x-height $\ge 6$ px on screen and $\ge 1.5$ mm in print, and its mark at the mark's minimum, so $H_\min = \max(6/(x s),\ 17.6)$ px:
  - horizontal: $H \ge 6/0.2371 = 25.3$ px, width $\ge 6.273 \times 25.3 = 158.7$, rounded up to **160 px**; in print $H \ge \max(1.5/0.2371,\ 7.35) = 7.35$ mm, width $\ge 46.1$, so **47 mm**
  - stacked: $H \ge 6/0.1395 = 43.0$ px, width $\ge 1.395 \times 43.0 = 60.0$, so **60 px**; in print $H \ge 10.75$ mm, width **15 mm**

## 5. Design Decisions

| Decision | Alternatives | Why |
|----------|--------------|-----|
| The brand's tools own the geometry (`art_geometry.py`) | Read the `art/src` SVGs | It matches the owner's other two kits ("edit the numbers in the script"), and the check holds 20 drawings to the app's sources byte for byte |
| A one-line wordmark in the horizontal lockup, one word per line in the stacked one | Two lines in both | One line reads as a name in headers; the stacked lockup's lines (0.20 H letters) come out about the mark's width |
| Compressed `.vectorcraft` sources | Plain JSON, as in `art/vc/` | 364 KB instead of about 2 MB for 27 files; gzip's timestamp is 0, so they stay reproducible |
| "© 2026 Mert Efe Şensoy" in the licence; the File Info names the product, not the person | The person's name in every file's metadata | PNG text chunks are Latin-1 and can't hold "Ş"; the licence file is UTF-8 |
| Shape with HarfBuzz from the font file | Install Baloo 2 into VectorCraft and outline there | Deterministic, offline, with no per-machine install |
| Commit Baloo 2 to `brand/tools/fonts/` with its `OFL.txt` | Download it at build time | Offline and reproducible; OFL allows redistribution with the licence |
| No preview in the EPS files | VectorCraft's default TIFF preview | The preview made a single mark 1 MB |
| Characters as SVG, PNG and source only | Every format | Nobody prints a mascot pose from EPS |
| No small mark; the favicon is the app icon (amended 2026-10-30, before the build) | A faceless small mark with the pages moved apart, as first planned | The approved identity sheet (C5) lists "pages pulled apart" under Never and gives sizes below 24 px to the app icon |
| C5's four versions and names; no black version | Cassandra's on-light / on-dark / black / white | The four are the approved set; black is off-palette ("recoloured off-palette" is on the Never list). The one-ink version is cocoa |

**Results (2026-10-30, this session, VectorCraft 0.18.0, Python 3.13, Node 24.20):**
- `build_brand.py`: 183 files in about 20 s.
- `check_brand.py --rebuild`: 183 of 183 files present and nothing extra; 20 drawings identical to the app's `art/src`; every SVG, PNG, EPS, PDF and source rule passed; contrast: the wordmark 11.70:1 (cocoa on milk), 15.84:1 (night milk on plum), 17.23:1 (white on plum), the tagline 5.69:1; `wcag.py` and `contrast.ts` agree on 57 pairs; **a fresh build matched all 183 files byte for byte.**
- **The check catches faults.** Five planted faults, each reported by name, then restored: a 256 px PNG in place of a 64 px one, a stray SVG, an EPS colour off by 0.009 in K, live `<text>` and an off-palette fill in a lockup SVG, and an unpinned PDF date.
- **Looked at, rendered:** the brand board, the construction, clear-space, palette and characters figures, the social card and the stacked lockup. After the first look the construction labels moved off the drawing, and the stacked lockup's letters went from 0.26 H to 0.20 H.
- **PDFs opened and rendered:** `guide/brand-board.pdf`, `pdf/valentine-pomodoro-mark-reversed.pdf` and `pdf/valentine-pomodoro-stacked-colour.pdf` draw as their PNGs do.
- **Not verified:** the EPS files were checked by their bytes, not opened in a print workflow; the CMYK values are unproofed. **Known limit:** the SVG colour rule reads six-digit `#RRGGBB` values only; VectorCraft writes those today, but a short `#fff` or a named colour would slip past it.

## 10. Amendment (owner, 2026-10-30): the colour version gets a cocoa cover

**Status:** done (2026-10-30); planned in `3112db4` before the change.

**Problem.** Looking at the kit, the owner said: "The Cocoa color when alone does not look appealing." The colour version is a large area of cocoa (`#4A2C29`: HSL lightness 23 %, saturation 29 %), a near-black brown block among pastels. Its white die-cut edge disappears on the light grounds it is made for, so nothing frames it. On the app icon the strawberry tile supplies the warmth; alone, nothing does.

**Options shown** (drawn with the mark's real geometry, on milk and white, at 200, 96, 48 and 24 px): A today; B blush cheeks; C strawberry pages in a cocoa cover; D two flavours (strawberry and matcha pages) in a cocoa cover; E a lighter "milk chocolate" `#7A4436`; F always on the strawberry tile. **The owner chose C.**

**The new colour version (C).** Cocoa moves from the fill to the frame: an open diary with a cocoa cover around strawberry pages.
- **Layers, back to front:** the white die-cut edge (each page stroked 100 wide with round joins, 50 outside the page, plus a 84 × 236 block at the spine); the cocoa cover (each page stroked 44 wide, 22 outside, plus a 60 × 226 block, so the gap between the pages' feet shows as a cocoa spine); the pages in strawberry; the faces in cocoa. The white band stays 28 wide, as on every other version.
- **Fit.** The wider outline measures 774 × 591 units against the frame's 784 × 576, so the whole drawing is scaled by $576/591.3 = 0.9741$ about its outline's centre (512, 534.35) and centred in the frame (512, 548). The frame, H, the lockups, the clear space and the minimum sizes don't change.
- **Colours:** cocoa (cover, faces), strawberry (pages), white (edge). Cocoa on milk is 11.70:1 and on white 12.47:1, so the silhouette reads on any light ground; the faces, cocoa on strawberry, are 6.76:1.
- **Unchanged:** the reversed version, the two single-colour versions (one ink can't print pink pages), the app icon (the owner kept it: the cocoa sticker on strawberry works there), Pomo & Doro.
